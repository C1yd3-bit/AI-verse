### Worker polling issue — found and subsequently corrected

**Initial finding: processing reconciliation could starve behind deletion candidates.** The worker originally selected the oldest ten `private_media_assets` with state `processing` on every invocation. Its queue helper created a deletion job without changing that asset state. Ten expired/hidden assets with retrying or exhausted provider deletions could therefore occupy the entire polling selection indefinitely. Newer ready assets whose webhooks were missed would never receive their canonical provider check and could expire instead.

The required correction was to exclude or durably transition queued/unavailable assets out of processing polling, use fair bounded polling for still-active records, and verify a batch larger than ten. It was a worker recovery issue; the previously reviewed deletion lease and token checks remained valid. Acceptance was withheld until the confirmation below.
